Playing. Click or touch the keys, or use the computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J play C to B, K O L P ; ' carry on up, and Z / X shift the octave. To use a real MIDI keyboard, press Connect MIDI (Chrome and Edge support this).

Choosing a tuning. Pick one on the desktop, or use the controls on the keyboard. Knob 1 (CC 74) steps through the tuning systems, knob 2 (CC 71) and the mod wheel pick the key, and pushing the pitch wheel up or down moves to the next or previous tuning system. Anchor. With Anchor C the keys are laid out as if you were in C and only the sound moves to the chosen key; Anchor A does the same with the white keys as A minor. Press the active anchor again, or None, to play the notes you press.
