Inspired by sacred geometry

Geometry, turned into sound

Shapes is inspired by sacred geometry, an art form of geometric patterns that appear in nature, science, art, religious symbols and cultures throughout history. This plugin turns those patterns into sound.

How it works

The display
The large display in the middle is a visualiser for the oscillators.
Transform slots
Each oscillator has six transform slots, three either side of the display. Each slot applies one transform, such as Twist, Ripple or Fold, and has two knobs.
Modulation
The mod envelope and the LFO can be used to change these geometric transformations over time. Press the + symbol on the mod env or LFO, click the knob you want to control, then set the amount.

Specifications

Oscillators
Two oscillators shaped by geometric transforms
Transforms
Six slots per oscillator, each with two knobs
Transform types
Pinch, Harm, Rip, Twist, WaveBend, Fold, Warp, PM, AM/RM, Polar Angle, Shear, Squash
Pitch
Coarse, fine and ratio, plus unison and vibrato
Filter
Lowpass 24, lowpass 12, highpass, bandpass, notch, comb
Modulation
Amp envelope, mod envelope and LFO, aimed at any knob with the + button
Polyphony
16 voices
Playing
On-screen keyboard, computer keyboard, pitch and mod wheels
